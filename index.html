📺 Your screen is made of tiny dots

Every screen — your TV, phone, this computer — is made of millions of tiny lights called pixels. Each pixel can glow red, green, or blue. When they mix, you see every color!

Each square above is one pixel. Move the sliders on the Challenges screen to change what color they glow.

🔢 Computers count from 0 to 255

Computers love numbers. For colors, each of R, G, and B gets a number from 0 (off / dark) to 255 (full blast). That's 256 choices for each, which gives us over 16 million possible colors!

256 × 256 × 256 = 16,777,216 colors. That's more than any box of crayons! 🖍️

💻 This is real code!

When coders style a website, they write exactly what you see in the game. For example, to make the background of a button red, a coder types:

background-color: rgb(255, 0, 0);
background-color: rgb(255, 140, 0); /* orange! */
Every color on every website was written as numbers just like these. You already know how to do it! 🚀
